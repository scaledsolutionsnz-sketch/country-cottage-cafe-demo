Our cottage

A proper country cafe on Mataura’s main street

Country Cottage Cafe is exactly what it sounds like: a warm little spot at 87 Main Street where the food is home-made, the coffee is taken seriously, and you’re greeted like a regular even on your first visit.

Locals drop in for the cheese rolls and cinnamon scrolls; travellers on the road between Gore and Invercargill stop for the wraps and a good flat white. Either way, you won’t leave hungry.

  • Locally owned and run, right here in Mataura
  • Everything in the cabinet is baked on site
  • Gluten-free options, both savoury and sweet
